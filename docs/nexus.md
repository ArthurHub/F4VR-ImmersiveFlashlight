# Short description

A highly versatile and configurable Pip-Boy flashlight replacement mod. Physically grab the flashlight off your body, hold it in hand, wear it on your head, or mount it on your weapon, with separate per-mode settings for beam intensity, distance, spread, color, and gobo. And now NPCs actually notice your beam.

# Full description

[size=3]A VR-first replacement for the Pip-Boy flashlight supporting in-hand, head-mounted, and weapon-mounted use. Each mode is independently configurable, providing precise control over beam intensity, distance, spread, color, and gobo. The flashlight is a physical object: grab it off your belt into your hand, bring your hand to your head to wear it as a cap lamp, or to your gun to mount it on the weapon. Location switching is physical and immersive, with sensible, context-aware restrictions. All configuration is performed in-game and applied live, with no reloads, restarts, or re-installs required.[/size]
[size=3]
Explore the wasteland at night with a powerful, long-range flashlight for visibility at a distance, then switch to a soft, wide head-mounted beam for close-quarters exploration and looting. And watch your back, because now the raiders can see your light too.[/size]

[size=4][b]Usage[/b][/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]Take the flashlight: Reach your hand to the flashlight model stowed on your chest and press trigger. Put it back the same way to turn it off.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Wear it on your head: Bring your off-hand close to your head until haptic feedback is felt, then tap.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Move it to your hand or weapon: Bring your off-hand close to your primary hand (or to the flashlight on your gun) until haptic feedback is felt, then tap.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Two-handed weapons: Tap the off-hand trigger while gripping the weapon to toggle the weapon-mounted light.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Classic toggle: The vanilla long-press of the off-hand trigger still turns the flashlight on/off. Turn it off in the config (misc screen, or [b]bDisableVanillaFlashlightToggle[/b] in the INI) so only the physical gestures control the light, for full immersion.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Configure settings: Open via FRIK configuration UI (hold both thumbsticks for 2 seconds).[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Every gesture and binding is configurable, and any gesture can be disabled.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3][b]Important: [/b]Set game "Shadows Quality" to "HIGH" to prevent corrupted shadows ([url=https://github.com/ArthurHub/F4VR-ImmersiveFlashlight/blob/main/docs/faq.md#the-flashlight-shadows-look-bad-or-blocky]details and how to disable shadows[/url]﻿).[/size][*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[size=3]Flashlight Modes:[/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]In-hand flashlight: Held in the player’s left or right hand, behaving like a handheld flashlight directed by hand movement, with a visible flashlight model in the hand.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Head-mounted flashlight (cap lamp): Mounted on the player’s head and following head orientation.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Weapon-mounted flashlight: Attached to non-melee weapons and aligned with weapon direction, including two-handed grip support.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Power Armor helmet lamp: Tuned separately, brighter and longer-ranged by default.[/size][*]
[/list]
[size=3]Beam Customization:[/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]Live, in-game tuning via a simple UI.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Per-mode configuration (+ separate for Power Armor head) allowing each flashlight type to behave differently:[/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]Intensity – brightness of the beam.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Distance – effective illumination range.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Spread – beam field of view.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Color – RGB beam color.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Gobo – beam texture / pattern.[/size][*]
[/list]
[*]
[/list]
[size=3]Immersion & Behavior:[/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]A physical flashlight model stowed on your chest that you grab into your hand and put back.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Physical, gesture-driven switching between head, hand, and weapon locations, with haptic feedback.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Two hand grip styles, forward (thumb-up) and overhand (ice-pick), detected automatically or locked to one.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Automatic switching:[/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]Primary hand to weapon-mounted when non-melee weapon is equipped.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Off-hand to weapon-mounted when gripping a weapon two-handed.[/size][*]
[/list]
[*]
[/list]
[size=3]NPC Light Detection:[/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]Vanilla ignores flashlights for stealth entirely. This mod makes your light count, and makes it directional: carrying a lit flashlight makes you somewhat visible, putting the beam on someone makes you much more so, and a bright beam in a hostile’s face at close range means they have seen you.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Line of sight is checked, so nothing is alerted through a wall, and NPCs investigate the spot your beam actually lights up.[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]On by default, fully tunable, and switched off with a single toggle ([url=https://github.com/ArthurHub/F4VR-ImmersiveFlashlight/blob/main/docs/npc-detection.md]details[/url]﻿).[/size][*]
[/list]
[size=3]Optional Restrictions:[/size]
[list]
[\*][size=3]Headgear requirement: Gate the head-mounted light behind worn headgear, either any headgear or only "light-capable" helmets, using a keyword and allow/deny lists you can extend ([url=https://github.com/ArthurHub/F4VR-ImmersiveFlashlight/blob/main/docs/headgear-restriction.md]details[/url]﻿).[/size][*]
[\*][size=3]Weapon flashlight requirement: Gate the weapon-mounted light behind a weapon that actually carries a modeled flashlight, and root the beam at that lamp. Auto-detects supported weapon mods such as Tactical Weapon Mods ([url=https://github.com/ArthurHub/F4VR-ImmersiveFlashlight/blob/main/docs/weapon-mount-restriction.md]details[/url]﻿).[/size][*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Advanced Configuration[/b][/size]
Most configuration is stored in "ImmersiveFlashlightVR.ini" file.
Much more than can be configured via in-game configuration interface.
Any changes made in the INI file will be live loaded into the running game to experience the effects.
Location:
[code]%USERPROFILE%\Documents\My Games\Fallout4VR\Mods_Config\ImmersiveFlashlightVR[/code]

[size=3]Incompatibilities[/size]
[list]

[\*][size=3][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/76586]Flashlight Stealth Fix[/url]﻿ solves the same vanilla problem as NPC Light Detection, so don't run both; uninstall it or turn off NPC Light Detection here.[/size][*]

[\*][size=3]Other flashlight mods: run only one flashlight replacement at a time.[/size][*]
